The JD Process

Five steps.
Zero guesswork.

A structured path from curious to confident owner, typically one to three months, at your pace.

Schedule My Free Consultation

Free consultation · No obligation

01

Initial call

A conversation, not a pitch. We learn your goals, capital, and timeline, and tell you honestly whether franchising fits. If it doesn't, we say so here.

02

Franchise assessment

A structured questionnaire turns your profile into a curated shortlist of 3 to 5 vetted brands, matched to you rather than to whoever markets hardest.

03

Brand conversations

We set up meetings with each franchisor and coach you through them: what to ask, what the answers actually mean, and what to watch for.

04

Due diligence & validation

Guided FDD review, item by item, plus structured calls with existing franchisees so you hear the unfiltered version before you commit.

05

A confident decision

You choose with clear eyes, or we go back and explore further. No pressure, no deadline. The right answer is the one that holds up in year five.

Built for serious owners, not window shoppers.

✓This is for you if

  • You want a proven system instead of betting everything on an untested idea.
  • You'll follow a disciplined vetting process before writing a check.
  • You're ready to actively run a business, at least in the early years.
  • You're a corporate professional, investor, or operator planning your next chapter.

✕This is not for you if

  • You're looking for passive, no-effort income.
  • You've already fallen in love with one brand and just want validation.
  • You expect a shortcut around due diligence.
  • You want someone to talk you into it. We'd rather talk you out of the wrong one.
